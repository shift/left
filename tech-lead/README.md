# The Promotion

> 📍 Late Game · 9 decisions made

Your manager leaves. Just... leaves. Two weeks notice, a box of succulents, and a Slack message: "You've got this. 💪"

You're now the tech lead. By default. No raise. Just a title change and a 1:1 with the CTO: "I trust you. Don't make me regret it."

---

*📖 This is called "accidental leadership" and it's the most common way tech leads are created. Google's Project Aristotle found that the best teams aren't led by the smartest person — they're led by whoever creates the most psychological safety. You didn't ask for this. That might make you the right person.*
