# The Debt Sprint

> 📍 Late Game · 8 decisions made · ⚠️ Warning

You spend the sprint paying down tech debt. Tests go from 12% to 34%. CI goes from 45 minutes to 12. You find and fix three bugs nobody knew about.

But the board meeting is next week. The CEO asks: "What did the team ship this sprint?" Your manager looks at the report: "Technical improvements."

The CEO's face does something it shouldn't.

*This is the hardest sell in software engineering.* Amazon's "two-pizza team" model worked because Bezos mandated that every team spend 30% of time on tech debt. Without a mandate, debt work is invisible to leadership.
